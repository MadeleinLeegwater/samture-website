Each principle of the Charter is written once as a check in an open standard, one your auditors can read and run again. The register is checked against all rules in one pass. Change a rule, or add a system, and everything is checked again: governance that can be verified, not only read.

What the pilot receives

The question is answered inside the foundation, where the offices’ records are joined. The pilot gets figures per district, never a row about a person. Groups smaller than five are withheld. The records stay with the offices, traceable on their side.

Also for Islamic finance: the order of steps

The ruling of the bank’s Internal Sharia Supervision Committee is held once, with its condition. Every murabaha is checked against it: did the bank take possession of the asset before it sold it?
